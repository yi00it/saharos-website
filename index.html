02The product

One platform. Every moving part.

Five tools that usually live in five different apps — built to share one source of truth.

01 — Schedule

A schedule the whole site believes.

Drag-and-drop Gantt with a real Critical Path engine underneath. Link activities finish-to-start, start-to-start, finish-to-finish or start-to-finish, add lag, and let Saharos do the arithmetic.

  • Critical path recalculated on every change
  • Baselines to show exactly how far you've drifted
  • Group by discipline or zone · day, week or month
  • Project calendars, holidays and PDF export
02 — Snags

Every defect, from photo to closed.

Snap it on site, note the location, set severity and assign it before you leave the floor. Nothing gets lost between the walk-through and the fix.

  • Up to 10 photos per snag
  • Open → Assigned → In progress → Review → Resolved
  • Install on any phone — no app store needed
  • Bulk import existing punch lists from Excel
03 — Command Center

Know where you stand before the first coffee.

One screen for what's late, what's next and what just happened — across every project you run.

  • Attention required, ranked by urgency
  • Deadlines for the week ahead
  • Live activity feed from every site
  • Schedule health and quality pulse
04 — War Rooms

Conversations that stay with the work.

Project channels, war rooms and direct messages that live right next to the schedule — not in someone's personal phone.

  • Threads, @mentions and reactions
  • Share drawings and site photos in context
  • Real-time notifications plus a daily digest
05 — Reports

Portfolio health on a single page.

Health scores, milestone status and snag trends for every project — ready before the board meeting starts.

  • Portfolio health score out of 100
  • At-risk projects surfaced automatically
  • Export to CSV or print to PDF

05Our story

We built Saharos because we needed it.

We didn't start as a software company. We started in the mud, managing complex sites just like yours.

We know the visceral pain of a missed detail. We've seen small communication gaps spiral into massive budget overruns. Spreadsheets weren't enough, and existing software felt like it was designed by people who had never worn a hard hat.

So we built the tool we wished we had — one that respects the complexity of construction but demands simplicity in execution.

“We'd rather be right than fast. Every feature has to remove confusion, not add it.”

06Questions

Straight answers.

What does “closed alpha” mean?

We're onboarding construction teams in small groups so we can work closely with each one and shape Saharos around how projects really run. Request access and we'll reach out personally when there's a spot.

Can I bring my existing schedule?

Yes. Import activities and snags from Excel (.xlsx or .xls). Add dependencies and Saharos calculates the critical path for you.

Does it work on site, on a phone?

Saharos runs in any modern browser and can be installed to a phone's home screen. Field teams can log snags with photos straight from the floor.

Which dependency types do you support?

Finish-to-start, start-to-start, finish-to-finish and start-to-finish, each with optional lag — plus project calendars and holidays so durations reflect real working days.

What languages does Saharos support?

English, Turkish, Russian and Azerbaijani. Each person chooses their own, so mixed teams can work side by side.
